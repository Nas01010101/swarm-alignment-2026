## Rubric (1–5 each, same across tracks)
| Dimension | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|
| Impact & innovation | Critical problem, genuinely novel approach or new direction, clear theory of change |
| Execution quality | Ambitious scope executed rigorously; surprising findings or unusually robust validation |
| Presentation & clarity | A model of how to present this kind of work |

Recommended structure: Introduction · Related Work · Methodology (replicable: models, prompts, sampling, metrics) ·
Results (variance + baselines) · Discussion (implications, limitations, future work).
