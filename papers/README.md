## Rules that apply to two papers
- Each paper is its own submission through the sprint form, with its own **distinct title**, its own PDF on the
  official template (≤8 pages + required Limitations and Dual-Use appendix), and its own author list.
- Each person can be on at most **two** projects, so all four of us can be on both.
- Both PDFs can link to this repo; each one names its folder (`papers/A`, `papers/B`) and the experiments it uses.
- The two papers must make **different claims**. A result goes in one paper's `claims.md`, not both. If B
  builds on A's library, B says so in its methods ("we reuse the detector from our companion submission").
- Each paper has its own `outline.md` and `claims.md`; every number in a PDF traces to a row there.
