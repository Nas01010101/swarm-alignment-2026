# Paper C outline: scarcity, reward hacking and a conservation barrier

Working title: *When Scarcity Breeds Subversion: Reward-Hacking Contagion and Control-Barrier Governance in
Mixed-Motive Agent Swarms*. Track 2 (science of agent networks), touching Tracks 1 and 4.
Experiments: E07 (baseline village, done), E08 (synthetic, N=200), E09 (E07 village + scarcity + loophole),
E09b (governance). Full spec: `papers/C/AGENTS.md`.

## Claim (one sentence)
Without a cheap way to game the score, LLM villagers work honestly under both economies (E07); with one, scarcity
drives discovery and spread of a ledger exploit that drains the real commons, and a content-free conservation
barrier at the gateway keeps the commons solvent where prompt rules and an LLM judge do not. (Everything after the
semicolon is a hypothesis until E08–E09b run.)
